Support Our Seaport: Pensacola

SOS Pensacola is a grassroots effort to educate the public on the value of the Port of Pensacola and garner support from the community as well as key business and political leaders. By doing so, the Port of Pensacola would gain higher vessel traffic, stimulate the local and regional economy and generate higher revenues in the Pensacola Bay Area.
